# Ghost data review

Reviewed 14 October 2026. BUSTBIBLE's 30 ghost records were compared with the linked community wiki revisions for evidence, speed, hunt threshold, and identifying tells. The latest official [v0.19.1.0 patch notes](https://kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-v01910-patch-notes) and [v0.19.0.0 notes](https://kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-v01900-quality-of-life-part-2) were checked for recent changes. This is a source review, not independent in-game testing or official confirmation of every mechanic. The wiki pages can contain unresolved bug reports and may change after the revisions below.

Corrections from this pass:

- Aswang: added the community wiki's 1.53 m/s base speed alongside its faster sight acceleration.
- Banshee: clarified that its 50% hunt threshold uses its target's sanity.
- Kormos: added the reported 70% threshold when a player sprints in its room and its movement-based hunt detection.
- Added dated source revision and patch context to every ghost card. Five cards also carry specific wiki-reported issue notes.
- Yurei door check clarified after a false positive: an ordinary slow interior-door close is a neutral note. The distinct [exit-door behavior](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Exit_Door) requires a front exit door to close outside a hunt or ghost event; [The Mimic](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mimic) can copy Yurei's ability. Ghost Writing plus D.O.T.S. excludes both under the documented evidence combinations.

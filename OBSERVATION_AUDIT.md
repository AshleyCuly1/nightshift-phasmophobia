# Observation coverage review

Reviewed 19 October 2026 against the linked Phasmophobia Wiki ghost pages, [hunt mechanics](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Hunt), [incense timing](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Incense), [fuse box mechanics](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Fuse_Box), and [identification guide](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Guides/Identifying_ghosts). The app now has 55 observations across the 30 ghosts in its roster, including a firelight blowout counter. This is a source review, not independent gameplay verification. Game patches, difficulty settings, cursed hunts, chance, and The Mimic can change how reliable a sighting is.

**How the app uses them:** A positive, narrowly defined confirmed tell can narrow Suspects. A probabilistic or conditional clue can only change their order. A broad note changes neither. Failing to see a behavior never excludes a ghost. Evidence-linked observations do not apply in zero-evidence mode.

The firelight tally and a burned crucifix are general notes. [Any ghost can blow out a firelight](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Firelight), and a [burned crucifix records a blocked hunt attempt](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Crucifix); neither alone identifies a ghost. The special Onryo clue requires an observed hunt attempt after the blowouts.

Power loss and flickering alone are also general notes. A [Jinn can trip the breaker by overloading lights](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Jinn), even though it cannot switch it off directly. An overload resets room light switches to off; a direct breaker interaction leaves their positions unchanged. [Hunts can flicker lights and switch them off when they end](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Light). The app only rules out Jinn when the player marks a direct ghost switch-off, and only rules out Hantu when the ghost switches the breaker on.

The ghost evidence combinations and selected mechanics in `data.js` were adapted and summarized from the community-maintained [Phasmophobia Wiki](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Evidence) and its [ghost identification guide](https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/wiki/Guides/Identifying_ghosts), available under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The descriptions here are newly written summaries, not copied guide text. Deildegast's addition was cross-checked against [Kinetic Games v0.18.0.0 patch notes](https://kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-v0-18-0-0-patch-notes). The 30 ghost pages and their revision IDs are listed in [DATA_AUDIT.md](DATA_AUDIT.md). The Wiki is community-maintained; game updates can change the mechanics. The latest official patch notes reviewed were [v0.19.1.0](https://kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-v01910-patch-notes).
